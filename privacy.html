Privacy

This site is run by Ashwini Kumar Mathur for the 10-Minute Worksheets course. In short: we keep only what is needed to save your progress, and only you and the teacher can see it.

What is stored

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email and profile photo.
  • The worksheets you tick as done, and when.
  • Your Like / Subscribe taps on this site (only totals are shown to others).
  • Messages you send from the Contact page.

Who can see it

You can see your own progress. The course teacher can see every student's progress and messages on a private dashboard. Nobody else can: access is enforced by the database rules.

Where it is kept

In Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Mumbai region). It is not sold or shared, there are no ads, and the site sets no tracking cookies of its own.

Videos

Lectures play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). When you press play, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Removing your data

Send a message from the Contact page asking for your data to be deleted, and it will be removed.
